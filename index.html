Where do I get the text?

The first edition (1939) of Alcoholics Anonymous is in the public domain in the United States, and plain-text copies are freely available online. Download one as a .txt file and import it here.

Later editions (2nd, 3rd and 4th) are still under copyright to A.A. World Services, so please do not import those unless you have permission.
